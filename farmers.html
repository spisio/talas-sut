Sell us your milk

We buy cow's and goat's milk all year and mare's milk in summer, from farms of any size in the Talas valley and the foothills. We collect it, test it and pay for it every ten days.

Getting started

How to start delivering

  1. Call the procurement office

    Ring 2-34-80-40 or ask at your nearest collection point. Tell us how many animals you milk and where you are.

  2. Vet visit

    Our vet checks the herd for brucellosis and tuberculosis and looks at your milking place. The visit is free.

  3. Sign the supply agreement

    One page, renewed every year. We lend cleaned churns to farms that need them.

  4. Deliver

    Bring your milk to the collection point morning and evening, or wait for the tanker if you are on a route. Every delivery is weighed and sampled, and you get a slip.

  5. Join the cooperative

    After a full year of deliveries you can become a member, vote at the April meeting and share in the year-end bonus.

Collection points

Eighteen in the valley

Our collection points are open 6:00–9:00 and 18:00–21:00, every day of the year including feast days. Each has a cooling tank, a scale and a sampling clerk.

The largest are at Orto-Ayyl, Semiz, Sary-Bulak and on the Kök-Bel road. Three more open at the foot of the summer pastures from May for mare's milk. Ask the procurement office for the point nearest you.

Services for suppliers

  • Free herd health visits twice a year
  • Artificial insemination at cost, with Holstein and Alatau bulls
  • Winter fodder on credit, repaid from milk deliveries
  • Milking hygiene courses each March, in Iradi and Russian
  • Churns and filter cloths on loan

Herding families

Summer contracts for mare's milk

For Ak Bee kymyz we sign contracts with herding families in April, before the herds go up. You keep and milk your mares as you always have; we lend cooled churns, collect daily from the foot of the pastures and pay by the litre every ten days.

We take fresh mare's milk only, not kymyz, so that every bottle ferments the same way. Contracts are limited by our bottling line, so families who delivered the previous summer are offered theirs first.
